Tips for faster cleaning and turnover

1. A “this is how it should look” photo

Photograph every room when it is perfectly prepared: how the bed is made, where the towels go, what's on the kitchen counter. The person cleaning then just compares, and the apartment looks like the listing every single time.

2. Two sets of bed linen and towels per bed

While one set is in the wash, the other is on the bed. Turnover then doesn't depend on whether the laundry has dried, which is the most common reason for delays with back-to-back bookings.

3. Supplies in one place

A locked cupboard or box with a stock of toilet paper, soap, coffee and bin bags means nobody has to run to the shop between two guests.

4. A photo after turnover

Five photos after cleaning take a minute, and in a dispute over damage or cleanliness they are your strongest evidence.

Frequently asked questions

How long does turnover between two guests take?

For a smaller apartment (studio or one-bedroom) usually 1.5 to 3 hours, depending on the state the guest left it in and whether the laundry is washed on site. With a checklist and supplies in one place the time shrinks noticeably.

Should I do the turnover myself or hire someone?

With one apartment many hosts clean themselves. As soon as you have back-to-back bookings or several units, hiring a cleaner pays off. That's exactly when a checklist keeps the quality the same as when you do it yourself.

What do guests complain about most in reviews?

Mostly small things: hair in the bathroom, dust under the bed, an unpleasant smell, missing basics (toilet paper, soap) and appliances that don't work. All of that is covered in the checklist above.
