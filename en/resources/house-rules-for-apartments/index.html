House rules for apartments: a free template

Ready-made house rules in Bosnian and English that you can copy, adapt to your apartment, send to guests or download as a PDF and print.

SmartStay team · Published 6 November 2026 · 6 min read

In short
  • House rules (also called guest rules) are the rules of the stay in an apartment: quiet hours, smoking, visitors, pets, check-out and responsibility for damage.
  • Good house rules are short (10–15 rules), polite and written in the guest's language.
  • Guests should see them before arrival, not only when they walk into the apartment.
  • Below is a ready-made template in Bosnian and English. Replace the orange parts with your own details.

What house rules should cover

House rules don't have to cover every possible situation. They should cover the things that actually happen and cost you the most time, money or goodwill with the neighbours. For most apartments that means:

  • Check-in and check-out: exact times and what guests do with the keys when leaving.
  • Quiet hours: the most common source of trouble in residential buildings. State the hours and the reason (neighbours).
  • Number of guests and visitors: who may stay overnight and whether visitors are allowed.
  • Smoking: where it is allowed and where it isn't. The smell of smoke in textiles means extra cleaning.
  • Pets: allowed, not allowed, or only by arrangement.
  • Waste and cleanliness: where the bins are. Guests genuinely don't know.
  • Damage: report it right away, and guests are responsible for it.
  • Contact: who to call when something is needed.

If your apartment has specifics (a fireplace, a jacuzzi, a boiler that needs switching on, a numbered parking space), add them as a separate rule or, better still, in the instructions for using the apartment.

How to write rules that don't sound strict

Guests came on holiday, not to school. House rules full of capital letters and words like “STRICTLY FORBIDDEN” make a bad first impression, and that often shows up in reviews. Three simple rules:

1. Explain the reason

People follow a rule more readily when they know why it exists.

Instead of

NOISE STRICTLY FORBIDDEN AFTER 10 PM!!!

Write

Please keep quiet between 10 PM and 7 AM — families with small children live in the building.

2. Say what guests can do, not only what they can't

Instead of

No smoking.

Write

No smoking inside the apartment, but there's an ashtray waiting for you on the balcony.

3. Keep it short

Ten to fifteen rules are enough. The longer the list, the smaller the chance a guest reads it to the end, and the important rules get lost among the trivial ones.

Where to put the house rules

The biggest mistake is house rules that guests only see once they enter the apartment. By then it's too late: the booking is paid and the guest feels ambushed. The rules should be seen several times:

  • In your Airbnb and Booking.com listing. Both platforms have a dedicated house rules field. Guests then see the key rules (smoking, pets, parties) before they book.
  • In the pre-arrival message. Send them together with the arrival instructions, a day or two before check-in.
  • In the apartment. A printed copy somewhere visible: on the door, the fridge or in a folder on the table.
  • In the digital guide. A link or QR code where guests can find the house rules, Wi-Fi and instructions at any moment, on their own phone and in their own language.

Frequently asked questions

Are house rules mandatory for an apartment?

Platforms like Airbnb and Booking.com ask you to state the basic rules in your listing (smoking, pets, parties, arrival and departure times). Even where they aren't mandatory, clear house rules protect you in a dispute over damage or noise, because the guest can't claim they didn't know.

Can I charge a guest for breaking the house rules?

Extra fees (e.g. for cleaning after smoking, or for damage) can only be claimed if they are clearly stated in the listing before booking and comply with the rules of the platform the guest booked through. If you plan to, state the amount in the listing and in the house rules in advance.

Can I download the house rules as a PDF?

Yes. Click “Print / PDF” above the template and choose “Save as PDF” in the print dialog. Pick the pets variant first; the orange parts can be edited after pasting into Word.

Which languages should the house rules be in?

At least Bosnian and English. If you have many guests from a particular country (e.g. Turkey, Germany or the Gulf states), it pays to add their language too. In a digital guide this happens automatically.

How many rules is too many?

Beyond 15–20 rules guests usually don't read to the end. Keep what actually happens in your apartment, and move appliance instructions (washing machine, boiler, TV) to a separate part of the guide.

What if a guest doesn't follow the house rules?

First remind them politely by message and point to the rule they received before arrival. If the problem continues or damage occurs, document it (messages, photos) and report it through the platform the guest booked on.
