FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do guests need to install an app?

No. Guests scan the QR code with their phone camera and the guide opens in the browser right away. No download, no sign-up.

How does the reception receive guest requests?

Requests arrive on the reception screen in the SmartStay app, with the room number, and in the reception's inbox. The reception clicks “Take” or “Done” and the guest sees the status in the guide.

Can individual rooms have different information, e.g. a different Wi-Fi?

Yes. All rooms follow the general hotel guide; for individual rooms or an annex you change only what differs — e.g. Wi-Fi or parking.

Which languages does it work in?

Guests choose Bosnian, English, German, Turkish or Italian. You write in Bosnian or English, and announcements and reception replies are translated automatically.

Does SmartStay replace our booking system?

No. SmartStay works alongside your existing system and handles communication with guests during their stay.

How much does it cost?

The price depends only on the number of rooms: up to 7 rooms 59 KM, 8–15 rooms 79 KM, 16–40 rooms 119 KM and 41–80 rooms 169 KM a month. All features are included in every package. Hotels with more than 80 rooms — by arrangement. The first 30 days are free, no credit card.
